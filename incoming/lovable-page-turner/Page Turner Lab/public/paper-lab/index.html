Paper Lab — one notebook, one history

An isolated R&D surface for replacing “a different paper preset on every page” with a small system of related paper conditions. Every sheet here is built from global notebook age + family/neighborhood + optional rare event, all seeded and deterministic. The production notebook is untouched.

AExisting damage vocabulary — review & cull

Classification of everything explored so far. Categories are decisions, not an inventory: the point of this section is to shrink the vocabulary before combining anything.

BThe two core families

Only two families remain: Carried / Handled and Protected Interior. Page 1 is not a family — it is the most exposed handled sheet. Humidity is not a family — it is an environmental episode with a radius (sections C and J). Same stock, same age, same notebook edge condition in every tile; what changes is surface history.

Controlled comparison — identical notebook edge, same geometry seed, no humidity, no events

Silhouette only — neutral paper, geometry alone

Surface history removed entirely. If these two read as substantially different paper, family edge influence is too strong and should be pulled back toward zero.

CDamage radius

The same run of six sheets rendered three ways: notebook-wide only, then with a neighbourhood event added, then with one individual-sheet event on top. Nothing else changes.

DGeometric edge wear

Identical paper material and colour in every tile — only the silhouette changes: edge recession, corner erosion, cockling, notches. Single-sheet geometry should stay restrained; the stack in section E is where it accumulates.

EFore-edge stack architecture

26 strata per study, anchored at the bound spine and accumulating at the fore edge. Depth is not a lateral fan: each stratum is a slightly different physical sheet — its own fore-edge and bottom extent (broad envelope + clustered deformation + small per-sheet deviation) plus its own recession, fray, chipping and cockle. All geometry is page-relative, so the thumbnail and the expanded view are the same object at two sizes. The second frame in each card magnifies the outer 30% of the block.

If the block still reads as old with the surface off, the geometry is doing the work.

FFamily sequences, exposure & the transition

Families are evaluated across neighbours, never on one page. Page 1 appears here as an exposure ramp inside Carried / Handled, not as a family. The last row is a gradual handled → protected transition; there should be no abrupt material switch.

JHumidity progression — one family, one episode

Carried / Handled throughout, notebook edge held constant, no rare events. Only the environmental neighbourhood rises, peaks and fades. The question this section exists to answer: can humidity own a whole chapter of the notebook without becoming a second species of paper?

KFull notebook flow

Fifteen neighbouring sheets moving from the front of the block inward: exposure falls, one damp episode rises and fades across a radius, and Handled gives way to Protected with no boundary. The edge language never changes. One notebook, one stack, one history.

GFamily + rare events

One family, six neighbours, two events. An event must read as something that happened to this paper — never as a different paper.

HExtreme reference study

Deliberately too much. Establishes the upper limit so the production target can be described as a fraction of it.

LNotebook comparison — A vs B
