Material Lab — wear & aging directions

Side-by-side comparison of materially different aging treatments for the notebook's exterior and its pages. Every sample is built the same way the notebook builds its surfaces: deterministic seeded canvas tiles baked to data URLs, stacked under layered CSS gradients — so any concept picked here transfers directly.

Nothing here touches the notebook. This is discovery only.

A · Wear archetype references

Historical leather studies, kept only as references for wear behaviour — rubbing, edge loss, humidity, scratching, cherished handling. The leather surface is no longer the direction; the wear stories are.

A2 · Cloth / board wear archetypes

The chosen substrate — the original Cloth / board aging recipe — carrying each of those wear stories. B01 is that exact material, untouched, as the baseline. Everything after it shares the same fibre substrate and differs only in wear placement, intensity, tonal drift and edge character. No weave, no leather. Click any sample to inspect it large.

A3 · Controlled wear comparison

Every sample below uses the exact same Cloth / board surface — same base colour, same fibre seed, density, scale and contrast, same title treatment. Only the wear treatment changes. A3-01 shows the frozen substrate with no wear at all.

dev check: — unchecked, all eight samples must be visually identical.

A4 · Wear synthesis

Same frozen Cloth / board substrate as A3 — only the combination of wear histories changes. Each concept blends the five preferred A3 families (handled, edge-worn, travelled, deeply loved, heavier wear) at deliberate weights rather than stacking everything at full strength. Overlapping corner abrasion, edge darkening, substrate exposure and scratch density are normalised so combinations stay plausible. Click any sample to inspect it large.

A4 sandbox · blend the wear families

Development tool, not final UI. Substrate stays frozen; only the weights move.

A4 · Color Study

Colour only. Every sample below is the same notebook — same frozen cloth/board substrate, same fibre seeds and scale, same title treatment, same Heavy but Believable A4 wear mix, same lighting and vignette. The single variable is the pigment the cloth was originally dyed with, plus the way that particular pigment ages: uneven dye absorption, sun-drift toward its own faded target, and neutral board showing through where wear has gone through the colour. Click any sample to inspect it large.

A6 · Finalist study — Charcoal × Brick

Broad colour exploration is closed. Two finalists remain — Charcoal (ages toward warm graphite / faded black) and Brick (ages toward dusty terracotta / clay). Same notebook, same cloth/board substrate, same wear system and presentation as A4; only the pigment and its colour-aware aging change. Stage 1 renders the strongest A4 wear concept (Heavy but Believable) in both pigments. Stage 2 puts the three strongest A4 wear concepts through both pigments as a final matrix.
